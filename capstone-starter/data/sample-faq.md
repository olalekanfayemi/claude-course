# Sample FAQ (replace with your own documents)

## Refunds
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are not available, but we can offer account credit.

## Support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm UK time. We reply to email within one business day.

## Cancelling
You can cancel at any time from the Billing page. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid month.

## Data and privacy
We store only the documents you upload and delete them within 14 days of cancellation. We never use your documents to train models.
